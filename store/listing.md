## Full description (4,000 max)

Billhead makes invoices on your phone and sends them to your customer, with no account, no subscription and nothing leaving your phone.

Set up your company once: your name or logo, address, usual terms, tax rate, how you want to be paid. After that every new invoice starts filled in, and the customers and items you have used before are a tap away.

Two ways to make an invoice. Ask me walks you through one question at a time: who it is for, what you did, when it is due. On the page puts the invoice itself in front of you; tap any part of it and type. Both end on the same invoice, and you can switch between them any time.

Simple or advanced. A simple invoice is one page: who, what, how much, one sales tax, a discount or a deposit, when it is due, how to pay. Advanced adds a purchase order, job site, service period, units, tax per line, freight, retainage, late fee terms, your signature and a customer acceptance line.

Projects billed more than once. Put an invoice on a project and bill it in stages: invoice 2 of 3, billed to date, remaining, retainage held, change orders in the contract. Each project page shows every invoice on it, what is paid and what is still owed.

Send it from your phone. One tap opens your email app with the PDF attached and the customer's address, subject and a short message filled in; press Send and it goes from your own address. Or share the PDF to any app, or save it to your phone.

Locked PDFs. The invoice goes out as a PDF your customer can print and pay but not change, with an Invoice ID that changes if the invoice changes. Sign it in the app with your finger if you want a signature on it.

Keep track. Draft, sent, overdue, partly paid, paid. Record payments as they come in, stamp an invoice paid, void a mistake without reusing its number, duplicate last month's invoice as this month's.

Any invoice under $100 is free, whichever kind, as many as you like. Above that, a simple invoice is 99 cents and an advanced one is $1.99, paid once when you download or send it; editing and sending it again costs nothing. Packs and a year pass come later. Billhead never takes a percentage of what your customer pays you. During launch, every invoice is free.

Your data stays on your phone. No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. When your phone's Google backup is on, Android backs the app up with the rest of your phone and brings it to your next one; a backup file under More is a second copy you control.
